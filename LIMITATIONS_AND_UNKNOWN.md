# Limitations and unknowns

- **Historical trajectory:** selected P-384 certificates are replayable, and the selected September 24 genotype and generator survive in private source. The original six `anneal_seed{101..106}.jsonl` files, their hashes, and every intermediate evaluation have not been recovered. Slack's six-seed tally is a reported aggregate, not a raw-log replay. The 424/423 certificate bytes were reconstructed from recorded recipes and matched to historical hashes; that does not reconstruct their original search runs.
- **Different episodes:** 425/424/423/422 were reached through differing constructions. The frozen 421 = 380S+41M came from CPU annealing. The later 421 = 381S+40M used as an OpenEvolve seed is a different certificate. Do not merge their histories or imply every milestone was a direct child of the prior one.
- **Evo engine evidence:** the sanitized OpenEvolve ledger replays arithmetic for 17 reported children, but private model responses and full engine logs are omitted. One trace row is missing from an eight-iteration batch. The compact case did not improve the seed. Adapter files for ShinkaEvolve or GEPA do not prove those engines ran.
- **Performance and generality:** operation count is not native execution time. Shortest/global optimality, current literature rank, side-channel review, upstream library adoption, and recipient acceptance are `UNKNOWN`. Other chain targets have separate baselines and mixed outcomes; do not describe the packet as a blanket multi-chain improvement.
- **Privacy and rights:** the chain archive carries its own license and third-party notices, including source-derived Curve448 material. The five new skills are MIT-licensed. The reconstructed search-history prose and separate case study are public for inspection with no reuse licenses granted. Brian's public article is attributed, but private correspondence is not bundled and no endorsement is claimed.
- **Access:** this repository is intended to be public. An authenticated owner view is not proof of recipient access; check the exact published files without signing in before sending a link. Publication is not recipient acceptance or endorsement.
